What worked

You opened with a strong recall question — asking three students by name about yesterday's content before introducing new material. That's exactly the kind of daily review that builds long-term retention.

A pattern to notice

Most responses stayed choral — the whole class answering together. That makes it hard to know which individual students actually understood. Checking for understanding was thin after your main explanation.

Try tomorrow

After your next explanation, pause for 5 seconds — then cold-call one student by name before moving on. Just one. It takes 20 seconds and tells you far more than "everyone understood?"
